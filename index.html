Our Story

Obsessed with great coffee since day one.

We started Awesome Coffee Beans because we couldn't find the kind of cup we wanted in Ireland. So we went searching — working directly with smallholder farmers, learning the craft of roasting, and building relationships that stretch from the slopes of Yirgacheffe to the mountains of Huila.

Today we roast every batch to order in our Dublin facility. No warehouse sitting. No stale beans. Just freshly roasted, expertly sourced coffee shipped directly to your door within 48 hours.

"The best coffee is the one that makes you stop and pay attention."
